This is where we get to know you and your vision. We'll discuss your lifestyle needs, budget, and potential property. Crucially, we dive into site specifics like land use planning, zoning, topography, and utility access. This ensures your project is feasible from the get-go and aligns with local regulations in San Diego County and Southern California. This foundational work prevents costly surprises down the line.

Once the property assessment is solid, we move into the creative phase. This involves one-on-one collaboration to develop your unique floor plan. Our 3D Design services include digital sketching, blueprints, architectural services, and code planning. We refine every detail to ensure the design perfectly matches your lifestyle and maximizes every square foot. This thorough design process sets the stage for a seamless build.

After your 3D design is finalized and approved, we use it to generate a precise build price. Once your deposit is placed, we immediately order all the initial materials needed for your home. This step is critical for staying on schedule, as some custom materials can have lead times. We work to secure high-quality components for your build.

This is where your vision truly takes shape! Our skilled team begins the physical construction of your tiny home. We pride ourselves on quality craftsmanship, building from the foundation up. While our typical build time is 90 days, custom homes with unique features or design changes might take 4-6 weeks longer. We keep you informed throughout this process, ensuring everything is built to your specifications and our high standards.

Once construction is complete, we conduct thorough inspections and apply all the finishing touches. This includes ensuring all systems are operational and every detail meets our rigorous quality checks. Finally, your beautiful, custom tiny home is ready for delivery and placement on your prepared site. You're ready to move into your perfectly designed, high-functioning small space!
