Navigating the Tiny Home Construction Process

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

Step 1: 3D Design Program (Initial 4-6 weeks) — This is where your vision truly takes shape. We work with you one-on-one to create a unique floor plan, including digital sketching, blueprints, and architectural services. This phase ensures every detail is perfect on paper before we cut any wood. Our experts also handle code planning and ensure the design aligns with local requirements.

Step 2: Material Order and Deposit (Week 6-8) — Once your 3D design is finalized and approved, a deposit is placed. This deposit allows us to order the initial materials needed for your home. We prioritize quality craftsmanship, ensuring only the best materials are selected to bring your design to life. The procurement process is carefully managed to avoid delays.

Step 3: Foundation and Site Preparation (Concurrent with materials) — While materials are being ordered, any necessary foundation work or site preparation begins. For homes on a foundation, additional engineering might be needed, which we're happy to assist with. Our site plan services cover land use, zoning, topography, and utility connections like power, water, and septic.

Step 4: Core Construction (Approx. 90 days for standard builds) — This is where your home starts to rise! Our skilled builders begin constructing your tiny home based on the approved blueprints. We're committed to delivering high-quality, custom homes. While our pre-designed models can be built in about 90 days, custom projects might take 4-6 weeks longer, especially if there are unique features or design changes.

Step 5: Interior Finishes and Utilities (Weeks 12-16+) — As the structure comes together, interior work kicks off. This includes installing appliances, cabinetry, flooring, and connecting all utilities. We pay close attention to every detail, ensuring the interior reflects your design and is fully functional. This phase brings your personalized space to life.
