What does a site feasibility consultation actually cover?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

Ever wonder what really happens during a site feasibility consultation for a tiny home? It’s not just a quick glance at your backyard! At Zen Tiny Homes, our consultations are super comprehensive, designed to give you a clear roadmap for placing your tiny home. We leverage our decade of experience and deep knowledge of Southern California's unique tiny-home landscape to cover all the bases.

Firstly, we dive deep into Zoning and Code Planning. This is a big one. We'll examine your property's specific zoning regulations to determine what kind of tiny home (like an ADU on a foundation or a mobile unit) is permissible and what restrictions apply. This includes setbacks, height limits, and occupancy rules. Zen Tiny Homes has worked extensively with local agencies, so we know how to navigate these complexities.

Next, we thoroughly assess the Land Use Planning and Topography. We're looking at the lay of your land – slopes, potential drainage issues, and the best spot for your home to maximize views and natural light. We also use aerial mapping to get a bird's-eye view of your property and its surroundings. This helps us identify the most practical and aesthetically pleasing placement.

Utilities are a huge part of the puzzle. We evaluate Power and Water Supply, as well as Drainage and Septic requirements. We check existing hookups, assess the feasibility of new connections, and plan for efficient waste management. This ensures your tiny home will be fully functional and comfortable.

Finally, we consider the Foundation Setup (if applicable) and overall access. Whether your home will be on a permanent foundation or a mobile unit, we’ll advise on the best approach. We also look at how construction crews and the tiny home itself will access the site, ensuring a smooth build and delivery process. Our goal is to move you beyond just dreaming of a tiny home to having a realistic, compliant, and personal plan.
