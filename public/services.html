Zen Tiny Homes is a San Diego–based custom tiny-home builder helping people create beautifully designed, practical homes that make more out of less space. Unlike traditional home builders, Zen begins with the realities of compact living: how the homeowner will cook, sleep, work, store belongings, entertain, access the home, and use the surrounding property. The result is a tiny home designed around daily life—not a reduced version of a conventional house. From the earliest property assessment through custom design and construction, Zen Tiny Homes works directly with each client to develop a home that reflects their lifestyle, land, priorities, and budget. Every project is an opportunity to rethink space: creating efficient layouts, meaningful storage, flexible living areas, inviting interiors, and a sense of openness that allows a smaller home to feel personal, usable, and complete. What truly sets Zen Tiny Homes apart is its experience at the intersection of design, construction, and tiny-home feasibility. The company has spent more than a decade building tiny homes and working with local agencies on pathways for legal tiny-home living across San Diego County, Los Angeles, Riverside, and surrounding Southern California communities. That experience helps clients move beyond the dream of a small home and toward a realistic plan for assessing their property, designing the right build, and navigating the unique considerations that come with tiny-home placement. Zen Tiny Homes is an American-owned small business committed to making high-quality, custom tiny living more accessible without sacrificing craftsmanship.
