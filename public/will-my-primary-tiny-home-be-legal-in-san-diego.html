Will My Primary Tiny Home Be Legal in San Diego?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

It's a really common and totally valid question to ask if your dream of a primary tiny home is actually legal here in San Diego. The answer, thankfully, is a resounding 'yes,' but it comes with some important nuances! We've spent over a decade navigating the ins and outs of tiny home regulations across San Diego County, so we really get where this confusion comes from. It’s not about if, but how and where.

One big thing to understand is the difference between a tiny home on a permanent foundation and one on wheels (often called a THOW). A foundation-built tiny home is typically treated like an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) here, and those are widely legal. If it's on wheels, it's usually considered personal property, much like an RV, and its legal placement as a primary residence can be trickier, often restricted to specific RV parks or designated tiny home communities.

The next crucial point is your property's location within San Diego. Rules can vary significantly between the City of San Diego and unincorporated San Diego County. Each has its own specific ordinances regarding size, setbacks, and utility hookups for ADUs.

Don't forget permitting! Even if tiny homes are legal, securing the proper permits is essential for a smooth and compliant build, and we're experts at guiding clients through this process.

We can help you understand the specific requirements for your property and ensure your project is fully compliant from day one.
