When should I get a professional site assessment for my tiny home?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

Thinking about a tiny home? That's awesome! Knowing when to call in the pros for a site feasibility consultation can save you so much time, money, and stress down the road. Here are some clear signs it's time to pick up the phone:

  • **You're unsure about local zoning or permits:** If the regulations feel like a maze, especially regarding ADUs or tiny homes on foundations versus wheels, professional guidance is crucial. Zen Tiny Homes has spent over a decade working with local San Diego and Southern California agencies, so we've got the lowdown.
  • **You've found a property, but something feels 'off':** Maybe the land has a steep slope, or there's an unusual drainage pattern. These subtle cues can become major hurdles without expert eyes on them.
  • **You want to connect to existing utilities, but aren't sure how:** Understanding the complexities of water, sewer/septic, and electrical hookups is essential. A professional can assess existing infrastructure and plan for new connections.
  • **You're trying to figure out the best placement for sunlight, views, or privacy:** A great tiny home is all about intentional design, and that starts with optimal placement on the land. Experts can help you visualize and plan for this.
  • **You're weighing options like an ADU vs. a mobile tiny home:** Zen Tiny Homes can clarify the foundational and legal differences, helping you choose the right path for your property and investment goals.
  • **You just want peace of mind:** Ultimately, a professional site assessment ensures your tiny home project starts on solid, compliant ground, turning your dream into a realistic, enjoyable journey. We're here to help you get it right from day one!
