Myths About Tiny Home Living as a Primary Residence

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

It’s easy to get caught up in the hype or misinformation about tiny homes. At Zen Tiny Homes, we’ve been building and advocating for these smarter living solutions for over a decade, so we've heard it all! Let's clear up some common misconceptions about making a tiny home your primary residence.

Myth: Tiny homes are just for temporary living or vacations.

Fact: While some tiny homes serve as retreats, many are designed as permanent primary residences. Zen Tiny Homes specializes in custom designs that integrate all the functionality of a traditional home, from full-size appliances to ample storage, making them perfectly suited for full-time living that’s intentional and comfortable.

Myth: All tiny homes are illegal or hard to permit.

Fact: Not at all! While regulations can be tricky, especially in Southern California, many tiny homes can be legally permitted, particularly when built on a foundation as an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU). We’ve worked extensively with San Diego city and county agencies to expand accessibility for tiny home living, and we guide our clients through every step of the permitting process.

Myth: You’ll feel constantly cramped and lack privacy.

Fact: Good design makes all the difference! Zen Tiny Homes focuses on creating efficient layouts, meaningful storage, flexible living areas, and inviting interiors. Our approach ensures a sense of openness, allowing a smaller home to feel personal, usable, and complete, not confined. We design around your life, so every square foot serves a purpose.

Myth: Tiny homes always mean sacrificing quality and comfort.

Fact: Absolutely not. Zen Tiny Homes is committed to making high-quality, custom tiny living accessible without sacrificing craftsmanship. We build homes from the ground up, ensuring every detail reflects superior quality and provides true comfort.
