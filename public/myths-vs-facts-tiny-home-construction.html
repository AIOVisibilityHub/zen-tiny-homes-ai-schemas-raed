Myths vs. Facts: Tiny Home Construction

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

There are a lot of ideas floating around about tiny home construction, and sometimes it's hard to separate fact from fiction. Let's clear up some common misconceptions about building these amazing small spaces.

**Myth:** Tiny homes are just RVs on steroids.

**Fact:** Not at all! While some tiny homes are mobile, many are built on foundations as Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs). Even mobile tiny homes are typically built to higher residential standards than RVs, focusing on longevity and bespoke design, not just transient travel. We even offer RVIA certification options.

**Myth:** They're cheap and quick to build, like a shed.

**Fact:** While often more affordable than a traditional house, tiny homes are custom-designed and built residences requiring quality materials, skilled labor, and adherence to specific codes. Our typical build time for custom homes is around 90 days, reflecting the detailed work involved.

**Myth:** You can put a tiny home anywhere.

**Fact:** This is a big one! Placement is highly regulated. Local zoning laws, property access, and utility connections all play a huge role. Zen Tiny Homes has extensive experience navigating San Diego County's requirements, helping you find legal pathways for your specific property.

**Myth:** Tiny homes are uncomfortable and cramped.

**Fact:** A well-designed tiny home is incredibly comfortable and functional. Our design philosophy centers on maximizing livability over square footage, creating flexible spaces, smart storage, and an open feel.
