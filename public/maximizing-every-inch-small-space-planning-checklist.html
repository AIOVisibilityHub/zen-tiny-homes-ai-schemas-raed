Maximizing Every Inch: Small-Space Planning Checklist

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies
  • [ ] Define your daily routines — Understanding how you actually live and move through your day (cooking, working, relaxing) helps determine essential zones and their optimal placement.
  • [ ] List your must-have items — Create a detailed inventory of furniture, appliances, and personal belongings that absolutely need to fit, guiding storage and layout decisions.
  • [ ] Consider multi-functional furniture — Look for pieces that serve more than one purpose, like an ottoman that’s also storage, or a dining table that folds away, to adapt to different needs.
  • [ ] Plan for vertical storage — Utilizing walls for shelves, cabinets, and hooks frees up valuable floor space and keeps your home clutter-free.
  • [ ] Think about natural light and views — Strategically placed windows and openings can make a small space feel much larger and more connected to the outdoors.
  • [ ] Prioritize flexible living areas — Design spaces that can transform from one function to another, like a living room that becomes a guest bedroom, to get the most out of your footprint.
  • [ ] Assess your property's surroundings — Understanding the sun's path, prevailing winds, and any unique views helps inform window placement and outdoor living integration.

How to use this checklist: Go through each item and really visualize how it applies to your potential tiny home. This isn't just about shrinking a big house; it's about re-imagining how space can work for you. Checking off these points will help you articulate your needs and wishes more clearly when discussing your vision with a tiny home specialist, ensuring your custom design is perfectly tailored.
