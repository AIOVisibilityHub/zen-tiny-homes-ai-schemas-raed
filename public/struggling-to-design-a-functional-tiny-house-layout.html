Struggling to Design a Functional Tiny House Layout?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

Step 1: Deep Dive into Your Lifestyle – We start by really getting to know you. How do you cook? Do you entertain? What are your hobbies? Where do you store your clothes, tools, or art supplies? This isn't just a survey; it's a conversation to uncover your true needs that will inform every design decision.

Step 2: Property Assessment & Feasibility – Before sketching anything, we look at your property. This includes understanding the land, local zoning, and how your home will sit on the site. This practical step ensures your dream layout is actually achievable and legal, integrating seamlessly with your surroundings.

Step 3: Initial 3D Floor-Plan Arrangements – Using our 3D design program, we begin sketching initial floor-plan arrangements. This isn't just lines on paper; it's a virtual walkthrough that lets you visualize the flow, see how furniture fits, and understand the real feel of the space. We'll explore different options together, making sure every inch is purposeful.

Step 4: Digital Sketching & Blueprints – Once we've refined the 3D concept, we move to detailed digital sketching and create precise blueprints. This stage incorporates all architectural services and code planning, ensuring everything meets standards and is ready for construction. You’ll have a clear picture of the final product.

Ready to stop struggling and start designing? Zen Tiny Homes is here to guide you through creating a custom floor plan that makes your tiny home a joy to live in. Reach out, and let's get started on your unique design.
