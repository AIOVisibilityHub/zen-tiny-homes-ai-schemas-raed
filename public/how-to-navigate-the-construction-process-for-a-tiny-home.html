How to navigate the construction process for a tiny home?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

Step 1: Initial Property Assessment & Design Consultation. This is where we kick things off! We'll look at your land, discuss your vision, budget, and how you live day-to-day. This helps us understand what your custom tiny home needs to achieve, from site-specific considerations to your personal lifestyle requirements.

Step 2: Custom 3D Design & Blueprints. Once we have a clear picture, our design team gets to work. We create a unique floor plan, digital sketches, and full blueprints, offering architectural services and code planning. This ensures your home is perfectly tailored to you and ready for the build phase. This usually takes a bit longer for custom projects than pre-designed ones.

Step 3: Pricing & Material Ordering. With your 3D design finalized, we'll give you a detailed price for your build. After your deposit is placed, we start ordering the initial materials needed for your home. This step is crucial for keeping the project on track and ensuring we have everything ready to go.

Step 4: Construction Begins! This is where your tiny home starts to take physical shape. Our skilled team handles everything from the foundation (if it's on-site) to the framing, exterior, and interior finishes. Zen Tiny Homes works hard to maintain quality craftsmanship throughout this entire phase, bringing your personalized design to life.

Our typical build timeline is around 90 days, but custom homes with unique features might take a bit longer. We work diligently to keep you informed every step of the way, ensuring a smooth and transparent construction process.
