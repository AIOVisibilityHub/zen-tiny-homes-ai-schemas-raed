Do You Know the Truth About Tiny Home Placement Regulations?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

There's a lot of chatter out there about tiny home legality, and it's easy to get mixed signals. Let's clear up some common myths:

  • Myth: Tiny homes are legal everywhere in San Diego County, so placement is easy.
  • Fact: While broadly legal, rules differ significantly between the City of San Diego and unincorporated County areas. Success depends on 'where' and 'how' it's built.
  • Myth: A tiny home on wheels (THOW) can be parked permanently anywhere.
  • Fact: A THOW is often considered personal property, like an RV. Its permanent placement options are far more restricted than a foundation-built ADU.
  • Myth: Tiny homes are always ADUs.
  • Fact: 'Tiny house' is a descriptive term for a small home. 'ADU' (Accessory Dwelling Unit) is a legal land-use classification for a permanent, foundation-built secondary housing unit. They're not interchangeable.
  • Myth: You don't need permits for a tiny home if it's small enough.
  • Fact: Almost all tiny home placements, especially foundation-built ones, require permits and adherence to building codes. Skipping this can lead to big problems.
  • Myth: Any builder can handle tiny home placement details.
  • Fact: This requires specialized knowledge of local zoning, utilities, and specific tiny home ordinances. Zen Tiny Homes has over a decade of experience navigating these complexities in Southern California. Don't risk costly mistakes; get the right advice.
