Are tiny homes really legal in San Diego?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

This is a question we hear constantly, and it’s totally understandable why there’s confusion! The short answer is a resounding 'yes,' tiny homes are broadly legal in San Diego. But here's the kicker: *how* they're legal really depends on where your property is located and exactly how the home is built.

It’s not a one-size-fits-all situation. The rules can be quite different between the City of San Diego and the unincorporated areas of San Diego County. The most critical factor is whether your tiny home is built on a permanent foundation or if it’s on wheels. A tiny home on a permanent foundation is often treated as an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU), which has specific zoning and permitting processes. These are legally tied to your property and build equity. If it's on wheels, like a Tiny House on Wheels (THOW), it's typically considered personal property, much like an RV, and has different regulations regarding where it can be parked and for how long.

Navigating these nuances is where experience really counts. At Zen Tiny Homes, we’ve spent years working directly with both city and county agencies in San Diego. This deep knowledge helps us guide clients through the permit process, ensuring their tiny home project is not only beautifully designed but also fully compliant with local regulations.
