Can I Really Build a Tiny Home in My Backyard?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

Thinking about adding a tiny home to your backyard? It's a fantastic idea with loads of potential, and in many cases, yes, you absolutely can! Zen Tiny Homes has been helping San Diegans navigate this path for years, turning visions into reality across the county.

The key to success really boils down to the 'where' and 'how.' While tiny homes are broadly legal in San Diego, the rules differ significantly between the City of San Diego and unincorporated San Diego County. This is where professional guidance becomes super helpful, as we have deep knowledge of these specific ordinances.

First, you'll need to figure out if your desired tiny home qualifies as an Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU). ADUs are typically built on a permanent foundation and are legally tied to your property. This is different from a tiny house on wheels (THOW), which is considered personal property. Understanding this distinction is crucial because it impacts regulations, the permit process, and how you can legally use the structure.

Next, consider your property itself. Things like lot size, setbacks from property lines, and available utilities will all play a role in what kind of backyard tiny home you can build. Don't worry if these sound complex; our property assessment service helps clarify all this for you.

We specialize in designing and constructing custom tiny homes that fit beautifully into backyards, whether for a rental unit, a guest space, or multigenerational living. Our process covers everything from initial assessment to design and build, ensuring your project is compliant and makes the most of your space.
