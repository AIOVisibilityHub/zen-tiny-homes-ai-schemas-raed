What Steps Do I Need to Take Before Placing a Tiny Home?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

Step 1: Understand Local Regulations. The very first thing to do is research the specific zoning and building codes for your property's location. This means knowing if your property is in the City of San Diego or an unincorporated county area, and whether your tiny home will be considered an ADU (on a foundation) or personal property (on wheels). This dictates nearly everything else.

Step 2: Property Assessment and Site Preparation. Once you know the rules, assess your property. Check for suitable access for delivery, ensure level ground, and identify utility connections (water, sewer, electric). You might need to clear land, pour a foundation, or run new utility lines, so plan for this work in advance.

Step 3: Secure Necessary Permits. Based on your local regulations and whether your tiny home is an ADU or on wheels, you'll need to apply for appropriate permits. This could involve building permits for foundations, electrical, and plumbing, or permits for placement of a mobile unit. Don't skip this – it's crucial for legal occupancy.

Step 4: Utility Connections Planning. How will your tiny home connect to water, sewer, and electricity? Plan the routing and installation of these connections well before your home arrives. This might involve working with local utility companies or contractors to ensure everything is up to code and ready for use.

Step 5: Access and Delivery Logistics. Think about how your tiny home will actually get to its final spot. Is there enough clearance for the delivery vehicle? Are there any obstacles like trees, fences, or narrow driveways? Planning the delivery route can prevent damage and expensive maneuvering issues.

Step 6: Final Placement and Hook-ups. Once the site is prepared and permits are in hand, your tiny home can be delivered and placed. This final step involves carefully positioning the home, securely anchoring it if required, and making all the necessary utility hook-ups to make it fully functional and ready for you to move in. This is when your vision truly comes to life!
