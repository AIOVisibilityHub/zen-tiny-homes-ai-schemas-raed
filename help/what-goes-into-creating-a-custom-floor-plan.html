What goes into creating a custom floor plan?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

Step 1: Initial Consultation & Lifestyle Mapping. This is where we deep-dive into your daily life. How do you cook, sleep, work, store belongings, and entertain? We don't just ask about rooms; we ask about *experiences*. This crucial step helps us map out a layout that truly supports your specific lifestyle, ensuring every inch is intentional and high-functioning, not just a scaled-down traditional house.

Step 2: Digital Sketching & Floor-plan Arrangements. Once we have a clear understanding of your needs, our 3D Design Program comes into play. We start with digital sketches and various floor-plan arrangements. This visual stage allows us to explore different configurations, showing you how space can be optimized for flow, natural light, and functionality. We ensure that our proposals incorporate efficient layouts and a sense of openness.

Step 3: Blueprint Development & Architectural Services. After finalizing the conceptual layout, we move into creating detailed blueprints. These aren't just pretty pictures; they're the technical drawings essential for construction. Our architectural services cover everything from structural elements to utility placement, ensuring your custom tiny home is not only beautiful but also structurally sound and meets all necessary specifications.

Step 4: Code Planning & Site Integration. This is a huge, often overlooked, piece of the puzzle. We actively work with city and county agencies to ensure your design complies with local regulations, especially here in San Diego County. This involves not just the home itself, but also how it integrates with your specific site—considering land use, zoning, power/water supply, and foundation setup. Our experience helps you navigate unique considerations that come with tiny-home placement.
