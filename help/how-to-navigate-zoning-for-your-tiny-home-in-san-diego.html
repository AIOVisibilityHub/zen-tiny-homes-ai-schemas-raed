How to Navigate Zoning for Your Tiny Home in San Diego

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

Step 1: Determine Your Property's Location — First things first, figure out if your property is within the City of San Diego or an unincorporated area of San Diego County. This distinction is crucial because the regulations can differ significantly. We can help you pinpoint this during a consultation.

Step 2: Understand ADU vs. Mobile Tiny Home — For foundation-built tiny homes, especially those serving as Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs), the City of San Diego generally treats them much like other permanent structures. Mobile tiny homes (THOWs) are often considered personal property and have different rules, sometimes more akin to RVs. Knowing this difference is vital for understanding what's permissible.

Step 3: Check Specific Zoning Codes — Each property has a specific zoning designation that dictates what can be built there. This includes factors like lot size minimums, setbacks from property lines, and maximum height. Our Site Plan Program includes detailed Zoning and Code analysis to ensure compliance.

Step 4: Consider Utility Hook-ups — Zoning often impacts how utilities (water, sewer/septic, electricity) can be connected to your tiny home. This needs to be factored into your site plan and budget.

Step 5: Get Professional Site Feasibility Consultation — This is where Zen Tiny Homes really steps in. We'll conduct a thorough assessment, covering land use planning, specific zoning codes, topography, and utility access. Our goal is to provide you with a clear, realistic plan for navigating all the requirements so your tiny home project proceeds smoothly and legally. Don't go it alone—we're here to help you make sense of it all!
