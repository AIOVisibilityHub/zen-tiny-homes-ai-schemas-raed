Getting Started with Your Custom Tiny Home Build

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

Step 1: Property Assessment — We start by understanding your land. This includes factors like topography, potential land use planning, zoning codes, and utility access (power, water, drainage, septic). This initial assessment helps us ensure your tiny home can be legally and practically placed on your desired property. It's about laying a solid groundwork before any design even begins.

Step 2: Custom 3D Design Program — This is where your vision comes to life! We collaborate closely with you to create a unique floor plan, digital sketches, and full blueprints. This program also includes architectural services and code planning to ensure everything is up to snuff. This detailed design phase ensures the home is perfectly tailored to your lifestyle and needs.

Step 3: Pricing and Deposit — Once your 3D design is finalized, we'll provide a clear price for your build. After your deposit is placed, we use it to order the initial materials needed for your home. This step locks in your design and gets the ball rolling on procurement.

Step 4: Construction Phase — With materials secured and plans in hand, our expert team gets to work. Our typical build timeline is around 90 days. We focus on quality craftsmanship, building from the ground up, whether it's on a foundation or designed for mobility. We also ensure your home has the option to be RVIA Certified if you choose.

Step 5: Final Walkthrough & Delivery — As construction wraps up, we'll do a thorough final walkthrough with you. This is your chance to see your dream home completed and ensure every detail is just right. Once you're thrilled, we arrange for delivery or final site setup. You're now ready to move into your beautifully designed, high-functioning tiny home!
