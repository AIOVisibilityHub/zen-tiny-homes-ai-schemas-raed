What to Consider for Your Tiny Home Site Plan?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

Getting your tiny home placement right is just as crucial as the design itself! A solid site plan ensures your home is not only legal but also functional and comfortable on your property. There are several key elements you'll want to consider, and at Zen Tiny Homes, we have a dedicated Site Plan Program to help you nail every detail.

  • [ ] **Land Use Planning** — This is foundational. You need to understand the designated use for your property and how a tiny home fits within that. It dictates what kind of structures are allowed and for what purpose.
  • [ ] **Zoning and Code Compliance** — Local zoning ordinances and building codes are non-negotiable. Whether you're in the City of San Diego or unincorporated County, regulations for tiny homes (especially ADUs) vary greatly. Checking these early avoids costly surprises later on.
  • [ ] **Topography** — The natural lay of your land affects everything from foundation requirements to drainage. Understanding slopes, flat areas, and elevation changes helps in positioning your home correctly and efficiently.
  • [ ] **Aerial Mapping** — This provides a bird's-eye view of your property, helping to visualize setbacks, existing structures, and potential access points for construction and daily living.
  • [ ] **Power & Water Supply** — How will your tiny home connect to utilities? Identifying the closest hookups for electricity and fresh water, or planning for off-grid solutions, is a critical step.
  • [ ] **Drainage and Septic** — Proper drainage is vital to prevent water damage and ensure sanitation. If you're not connecting to a municipal sewer, you'll need to plan for a septic system or other waste management.
  • [ ] **Foundation Setup** — Will your tiny home be on a permanent foundation or wheels? This decision profoundly impacts the site plan, engineering needs, and legal classification. Our team can help with additional engineering work for foundation-based homes.

How to use this checklist: This checklist is your starting point for a successful tiny home placement. Go through each item and consider how it applies to your specific property. If you find yourself scratching your head on any of these, especially regarding San Diego’s unique local regulations, it’s a clear sign that professional help can save you a lot of time and potential headaches. Zen Tiny Homes specializes in navigating these exact details to make your placement plan seamless.
