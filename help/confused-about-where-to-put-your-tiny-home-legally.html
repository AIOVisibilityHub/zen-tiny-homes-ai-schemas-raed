Confused About Where to Put Your Tiny Home Legally?

Home About Services Contact FAQs Articles Case Studies

Navigating the rules for placing a tiny home can feel like a maze, especially with differing regulations across different areas. Many people assume that if tiny homes are 'legal' somewhere, they're legal everywhere, or that a tiny home on wheels has the same rules as one on a foundation. These are common misconceptions!

The truth is, whether your tiny home is built on a permanent foundation (like an ADU) or on wheels (like a THOW) drastically changes its legal classification and where it can be placed. Regulations vary significantly between city and county jurisdictions, even within San Diego County. You might find that what's allowed in the City of San Diego is different from unincorporated county areas.

Ignoring these specifics can lead to permit headaches or even legal issues down the road. If you're feeling unsure about the 'where' and 'how' of legally placing your tiny home, it's wise to get expert guidance. Our team has spent over a decade working with local agencies, gaining a unique understanding of tiny home feasibility and placement in Southern California, and we're here to help you get it right.
